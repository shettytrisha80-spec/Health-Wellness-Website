💪 Physical Health Tips

Exercise Daily

Move your body at least 30 minutes a day to boost energy and prevent disease.

Hydrate

Drink enough water to support metabolism and flush out toxins.

Sleep Well

Rest 7–9 hours per night to recharge your mind and body.

🧠 Mental Health Tips

Mindfulness

Practice deep breathing or meditation to reduce stress and anxiety.

Connect Socially

Stay connected with loved ones to combat loneliness and boost mood.

Digital Break

Step away from screens regularly to protect your mental focus and vision.

🥗 Healthy Diet Tips

Eat Fresh

Choose fruits, vegetables, and whole foods to fuel your body.

Avoid Junk

Minimize processed snacks and sugar-rich drinks for better digestion.

Balanced Meals

Combine carbs, proteins, and healthy fats in every meal.
